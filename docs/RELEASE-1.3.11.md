# Snapir Design X 1.3.11 — Snapshot

## The skirting is built

It was found, measured, counted and reported — six boards on this wall, 6.4 cm
tall, 2.2 cm proud — and never once built. `pervaz` appeared nowhere in the
solid builder. Every room came back with a plain wall meeting a plain floor,
while the app said it had found the boards.

The board is not added to the wall; it is what is left when the wall above it
steps back. The surveyor's two runs say exactly where that is: the outer line
at floor level is the face of the board, and the top of the board a few
centimetres up and a couple further out is the wall behind it. So the wall is
taken back to its own face from the top of the board to the ceiling, and the
ledge that leaves is the skirting.

`Daire 45 - Oda` carries its board now. Sectioned at 3 cm the room holds
2.853 m² of wall; at 30 cm and at 120 cm it holds 2.258 m². That step is the
board.

## Checked

Both cores build it identically — one solid, 39 faces, 11.868040439 m³ and 140
triangles on either side, agreeing to nine decimal places.

A first attempt ran the band past the ceiling and took the slab with it: three
cubic metres and half the faces of the room. There is a test for that now.
