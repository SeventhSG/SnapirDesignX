# Snapir Design X 1.3.6 — Snapshot

## Round or square, and you decide which

A boiler was the only thing that ever came out round, because the shape was
wired to the name. Plenty of things on a wall are cylindrical and are not
boilers, and plenty of boilers are square.

The shape is its own choice now — **Extruded as: Square / Round** in the
inspector, on any rectangle that is not a hole. It defaults the way it always
behaved, a boiler round and everything else square, so nothing that was right
before changes on its own.

A round fitting reaches the depth shot exactly as a square one does: the
rectangle gives the diameter, the shot says how far it stands out. A round
recess bores into the wall rather than standing in it.

## Checked

Six rooms, both cores, zero mismatches across 226 parsed fields and 72 built
ones. A round fitting and a square one both land their front face on the shot
to within a hundredth of a centimetre, and the cylinder carries less material
than the box that contains it — which is the arithmetic saying it really is
round.
