# Snapir Design X 1.3.7 — Snapshot

## Add the point you missed, from inside the room

Some corners only announce themselves once the room is built and you are
standing in it. Until now the only answer was to go back to the building.

Stand inside, turn on **Add point**, and a crosshair appears in the middle of
the view. Turn the panorama on to see what is really there, aim at the spot,
and put a point on it. Where the crosshair meets the body is where the point
lands, in the survey's own coordinates.

The crosshair sits dead centre rather than following a finger, because on a
tablet a fingertip covers the very thing being aimed at. Only the button takes
a tap; looking around still works exactly as it did.

A point added this way is **constructed, not measured**. It carries that
plainly — marked as derived, with "added from inside the room" recorded
against it — and it arrives unclassified, for you to say what it is. Nothing
about it can be mistaken later for something the instrument shot.

## Checked

The scene and survey transforms are exact inverses, which is what decides
whether a point lands where it was aimed: five positions round-tripped with a
worst error of one ten-trillionth of a centimetre.

Through the shipping service, a point added this way comes back at exactly the
coordinates it was given, flagged as derived and carrying how it was made.
