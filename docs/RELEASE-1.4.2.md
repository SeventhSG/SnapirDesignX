# Snapir Design X 1.4.2 — The merger stands up

The sketch merger drew every room flat. A stairwell is the one thing a plan
cannot show: its floors sit on top of each other, so drawn flat they land in
the same place on the paper and the corner you are reaching for is under three
other corners.

It is a 3D scene now. Every room is drawn at its own height, in its own colour:
the frame room where it was surveyed, matched rooms where they were solved to —
so a floor matched to the one below it visibly sits on the one below it — and
rooms nobody has matched parked to one side, faded, but still there to be
clicked.

Orbit, zoom and **Fit** as anywhere else in the app. Clicking a corner or a
line does what it did: one click in one room, one in another, and that is a
match. Nothing about the solving changed, only what you are looking at while
you do it.

## Checked

Driven in a browser rather than assumed: open the merger on
`Sofia - Villa Tocheva`, and the scene comes up with all four rooms, two of
them matched and stacked at their surveyed heights, and a click on the canvas
picks the corner or the line under it. 104 tests, of which 100 run here.
